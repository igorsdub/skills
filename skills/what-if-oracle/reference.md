## What it does

`what-if-oracle` guides structured scenario planning, contingency design, and stress-testing for research projects and experimental designs under uncertainty.

Key capabilities include:
- **Operational Framing:** Formulates precise operational research questions specifying decision variables, perturbations, horizons, measurable outcomes, and baseline empirical data.
- **Evidence Ledgers:** Maintains a disciplined separation between observed experimental data, model-derived projections, and unverified planning assumptions.
- **Multi-Branch Exploration:** Examines six scenario lenses:
  - *Favorable:* conditions improve within justified bounds.
  - *Reference:* stated baseline or central case assumptions.
  - *Adverse:* plausible experimental failures and cascading risks.
  - *Wild Card:* external disruptions outside standard operational assumptions.
  - *Contrarian:* alternative explanations or competing scientific hypotheses.
  - *Second Order:* downstream effects, delays, and behavioral feedback loops.
- **Actionable Decision Triggers:** Identifies early-warning indicators and concrete contingency paths for each branch before research commitments are locked.

## Common questions

**Does this skill generate calibrated probabilities or statistical forecasts?**

No. Scenario branches are structured exploration lenses, not a mutually exclusive probability distribution or an empirical forecasting engine. They help identify blind spots and plan contingencies, but they do not prove that a narrated mechanism will occur.

**When should I use this skill instead of a computational model?**

Use `what-if-oracle` during early planning or protocol design when defining variables, identifying vulnerable assumptions, or preparing mitigation plans. When empirical parameters and mathematical models exist, follow up with formal statistical modeling or computational simulations.

**What is the purpose of the evidence ledger?**

The evidence ledger categorizes every premise as observed, model-derived, or assumed. This prevents teams from mistaking comfortable planning assumptions for verified empirical facts when constructing scenarios.

**Are external APIs or dependencies needed?**

No. The qualitative scenario analysis framework runs completely offline with no software dependencies, external network calls, or API keys.

## It's working if

- The research question specifies clear comparator conditions, time horizons, and measurable success criteria.
- Observed data points are explicitly documented separately from assumptions.
- Scenarios explore adverse failure modes and contrarian mechanisms rather than only optimistic paths.
- Each branch outlines early-warning indicators and a concrete contingency action.
- Scenario summaries clarify that qualitative stress-testing does not constitute calibrated probabilistic prediction.
